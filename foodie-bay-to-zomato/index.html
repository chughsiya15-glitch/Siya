The craving

It’s 11:30 at night, you are sitting with your friends enjoying the movie and suddenly you all crave food, especially bhel, because bhel is delivered faster than bail. But you don’t have the raw materials for it.

You don’t want to step out to get food; instead you want it to be delivered to your doorstep. So what is that one thing that comes to your mind when someone says let’s order food online? Zomato, right?

Is it even possible that you don’t think about this name? No, right? Zomato appears as a genie whenever someone thinks of food. But what is the story behind Zomato and how did it become such a hunger genie?

How it started

Back in 2008, the founders of Zomato, Pankaj Chaddah and Deepinder Goyal, were well aware of the demands of the consumers and they knew how to scale it. People crave good food, whether at home or in the office. But either they don’t have time for cooking or they’re just too lazy to go out.

At the same time, the development of innovative technology like food delivery applications gave wings to their dreams. So while working at Bain & Co, a consulting firm in Delhi, they saw a lot of people waiting in long queues just to have a glance at the menu pad to order food and even to pick up the food.

They came up with the solution of launching a directory of restaurants online. They went restaurant hopping to collect all the menu cards and uploaded the soft copies of the menu cards on the website for the ease of their fellow workers.

By doing this, they naturally saved a lot of money and a lot of time, and at the same time the website became incredibly popular. So they named their website Foodie Bay, which started in Delhi and expanded to Mumbai, Calcutta, and even Bangalore.

Why the name changed

But 2010 became a turning point as they thought of renaming Foodie Bay. As branding plays an important role and they wanted to touch broader horizons, Foodie Bay wasn’t the best option for them to go with as it might confine one’s perception to that of being just a food website.

Also, people confused Foodie Bay with eBay, so they had to change the name. Thus arrived Zomato, the word which rhymed with tomato. Tomato is easily identifiable with food, so Zomato. The Z, says Deepinder Goyal, adds an extra zing to the title, and so the name.

How Zomato earns

Apart from this, Zomato now charges commissions from the restaurants so that they can be prominently put on the list. The restaurants can even pay for promoting their events or deals as well as their overall banner, which brings increased visibility and conversions from the users of Zomato.

The marketing playbook

Zomato’s marketing strategy is a delightful blend of creativity, technology, and customer-centric initiatives that have driven the platform to the top of the food industry.

  1. Technology.

    Zomato’s tech-savvy approach is a fundamental component of their marketing strategy. The platform makes use of cutting-edge technologies, including a smooth user interface that accommodates the wide range of tastes of its audience and AI-driven personalized suggestions. Zomato predicts its users’ food cravings and understands them by combining data analytics and machine learning to provide a fun and customized experience.

  2. Storytelling.

    Zomato shines in the storytelling genre, even above the limits of pixels and algorithms. By telling the tales of the foods, the eateries, and the people who make them possible, the brand engages its audience on an emotional level. Zomato invites users to go on a culinary journey with every order by transforming food into an experience through captivating content and eye-catching advertising.

  3. Community.

    Zomato’s marketing approach goes beyond the internet to foster a thriving food community. User-generated material is encouraged on the platform, including food images, recommendations, reviews, and ratings. Zomato increases its reach and impact in the fierce food sector by turning its users into brand ambassadors through the development of a feeling of community.

  4. SEO.

    Zomato puts a lot of effort into content, but its SEO strategies are no less amazing. SEO is more of an amplifier than a channel, and Zomato understands the groundwork well. SEO, ultimately, is all about creating content focused on users but optimized for search engines.

  5. Innovation.

    Zomato’s dedication to innovation is one of its most notable qualities. Zomato is a leader in the market, having introduced new services like cloud kitchens and embraced cutting-edge technology like augmented reality (AR) for menu research. Zomato makes sure that its marketing approach is current and engaging to a wide range of consumers by continuously reimagining the dining experience.

The takeaway

One thing is certain as Zomato develops and grows its food empire: its marketing approach is just as alluring as the food it serves. Bon appétit!
